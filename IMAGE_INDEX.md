## How to use this index

Descriptions record visible content, not verified campaign biography. The headshot is attributed to Julia by the user’s instruction and filename. Other people are described without face-based identity claims; confirm which participant is Julia before publishing a named caption or alt text. Event dates, relationships, organizations, roles, and endorsements are not established by appearance. Suggested alt text below is a draft to adapt to the selected crop and surrounding copy.

The uploaded photos’ original URLs, photographers, dates, licenses, and caption approvals were not supplied. Social-style filenames do not establish provenance. No external source has been matched to these files. Subject-matter tags classify visible scenes; they do not establish campaign positions.

Keep original filenames and originals when making optimized derivatives. Each record includes dimensions, file size, and a SHA-256 fingerprint: unchanged fingerprints mean these observations can be reused; review only new or changed files. All 22 fingerprints are distinct. Similar scenes are noted below.

## Cutline guidance

Cutlines follow [Ward’s Writing Cutlines handout](public/images/Ward-cutlines_handout.pdf), especially its accuracy guidance (page 2), extended-cutline structure (page 3), and short identification cutlines for accompanying stories (page 4). Alt text describes an image for accessibility; a cutline identifies its subjects and adds verified context for readers.

Records 1–19 contain **working extended cutlines**, with present-tense action first and a past-tense context/date sentence second. Bracketed text is missing reporting, not a fact or publishable copy. These drafts must be completed against source captions or information supplied by Julia; unknown details cannot be recovered from faces, clothing, backdrops, or filenames. The headshot uses a short identification cutline because it accompanies an introduction rather than a news event. Avoid adding praise, inferred feelings, endorsements, or invented outcomes. Recheck all named people and left/right references after cropping. Add the verified photographer/source credit separately to every photo before publication.
