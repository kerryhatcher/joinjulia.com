## Product Purpose

The supplied brief describes her as running for Bibb County Democratic Party
Chair in Macon, Georgia. Its central message is that it is time for a change
and time to make things happen.

The user confirmed the primary visitor goal: read Julia's story and campaign
priorities. “Explore the priorities” is the main homepage action; existing
social profiles provide the connection action.
